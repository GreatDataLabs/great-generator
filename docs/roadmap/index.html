Roadmap

Roadmap items are not guarantees. They are visible areas for future contribution and planning.

Great Generator creates synthetic data. It does not anonymize, mask, de-identify, or transform production records.

Near-term ideas

Potential future work includes JSON Schema support, YAML profiles, Pydantic and dataclass adapters, Spark-native query-aware generation, and more quality-tool examples.

Contributions

Issues, discussions, docs examples, recipes, and domain-pack suggestions are useful ways to contribute.
