# Original Contribution Evidence Guide

This is an evidence-organization guide, not legal advice and not an immigration eligibility conclusion.

Record four categories separately:

1. **Authorship:** repository history, release artifacts, design records, and dated technical writing.
2. **Technical originality:** prior-art comparison, precise method description, experiments, and independent replication.
3. **Technical contribution:** released implementation, tests, reproducibility artifacts, and demonstrated problem-solving capability.
4. **Independent significance:** third-party evaluations, integrations, publications, or documented adoption from sources independent of the author.

Software publication, a benchmark, a paper, a patent, downloads, stars, or forks do not automatically establish originality or major significance. Preserve primary evidence, describe limitations, and obtain qualified legal advice for any legal filing.
