# Impact Evidence Checklist

- [ ] Claim identifies a specific technical contribution.
- [ ] Source is public or preserved with permission.
- [ ] Source is independent where independence is claimed.
- [ ] Version, date, and environment are recorded.
- [ ] Measurement method and denominator are stated.
- [ ] Raw evidence can be reproduced or inspected.
- [ ] Limitations and unsuccessful results are included.
- [ ] Automated activity is separated from human activity.
- [ ] Interest metrics are not presented as proof of significance.
- [ ] Personal, confidential, or production data is excluded.
- [ ] Disclosure permission is recorded where required.
